    🔋 Thermal Management

    Helps maintain a more stable simulated charging temperature.

    How It Works

    1. ChargingThe phone receives power from the charger.
    2. Temperature MonitoringThe temperature sensor continuously monitors the phone.
    3. Heat DetectionIf the simulated temperature becomes high, the microcontroller detects it.
    4. CoolingThe cooling fan and heat sink activate.
    5. StabilizationThe temperature decreases and the system returns to a safe simulated operating state.

    This is an educational prototype. Temperature values, charging behavior, and cooling performance are simulated and do not represent certified smartphone safety limits or a production charging system.
